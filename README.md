# VEX V5 Competition 2024

This repository contains a collection of my work and contributions to the VEX V5 Competition 2024. I participated as a supporting member of the main competition team, contributing to the engineering documentation, mechanical design, CAD development, and technical calculations.

## My Contributions

My primary responsibilities included:

* Engineering Notebook

  * Documented the team's design process, development progress, testing results, and engineering decisions.
  * Organized technical information and maintained documentation throughout the development process.

* Physics and Engineering Calculations

  * Performed physics-based calculations to support robot design and performance.
  * Analyzed mechanical systems and applied calculations to improve design decisions.

* CAD Design

  * Designed and developed robot components and assemblies using CAD software.
  * Considered mechanical constraints, component dimensions, and assembly requirements during the design process.

* 3D Design and Modeling

  * Created 3D models and technical drawings of robot components.
  * Developed models to support prototyping, fabrication, and design validation.
